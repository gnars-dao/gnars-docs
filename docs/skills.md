## Revenue Streams

1. **Membership Auctions** — Continuous NFT auctions on nouns.build; 100% to treasury (minus protocol fee)
2. **Droposals** — Governance proposals with onchain NFT mints; revenue split between DAO and creators
3. **$GNARS Token** — Creator coin on Zora backed by community content (under development)
4. **Physical Products** — Merchandise and collaborations (coming soon)

## Governance Parameters

- **Auction duration**: 1h 20m
- **Reserve price**: 0.01 ETH
- **Proposal threshold**: 0.2% of total votes
- **Quorum threshold**: 10% of total "For" votes
- **Founder allocation**: 0%
- **Voting power**: 1 GNAR = 1 VOTE

## What Gnars DAO Does

- Funds skatepark refurbishments and action sports infrastructure worldwide
- Installs "Nounstacle" sculptures (skateable Noggles-shaped obstacles) in cities globally
- Supports athletes and creators through onchain content minting and rewards
- Operates decentralized governance where NFT holders vote on treasury spending
- Bridges action sports culture with web3 through creator coins, NFTs, and token incentives
